Written 2026-11-22. No src change. All video numbers are ESTIMATES (error about +-25 %): the video has no dimension plates and no published drawing was found.

## 1. What the video shows (shot_50s.jpg, shot_2s.jpg, shot_80s.jpg)
- ONE through highway on a DIAGONAL FLYOVER; the ground cross road passes under it with one-way C-D roads on both sides.
- FOUR loop ramps, one per quadrant. Two loops hold a round pond with a ring of trees; two hold a big oval lawn with coloured stripe rings.
- Outer ramps / slips between the loops, triangular lawns with trees between the ramps, a lake and buildings at the east, a service road along the south edge.
- Flyover: 2 + 2 lanes + median, kerb and railing. Loop ramps: single lane, outer barrier, lamp poles (night views).

## 2. Scale method
Flyover deck read as about 24 m wide in the top view; other lengths measured against it. Result: pond loops R about 55 - 65 m, lawn loops R about 100 - 130 m, whole interchange about 600 - 700 m across. ESTIMATE, +-25 %.
